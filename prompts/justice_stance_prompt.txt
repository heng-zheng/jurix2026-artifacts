Aggregation rules:
- Support toward at least one part and no opposition anywhere in the bundle: Favor.
- Opposition toward at least one part and no support anywhere in the bundle: Against.
- Neither support nor opposition toward any part: Neutral.
- Support toward one part and opposition toward another part: Mixed.
- Favor plus Neutral is Favor; Against plus Neutral is Against; only Favor plus Against is Mixed.
